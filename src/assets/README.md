# Assets

Artwork, logo, fonts and video links for the Grand Theft Developer portfolio. All artwork is our own (AI-generated); nothing here is Rockstar's.

Every file here is used. `source/` and `brand/` hold the originals, `site/` holds the images the page loads (generated from them), and `hosted-media.json` points at the videos on Cloudinary. Folders are named after the page section they belong to.

## Changing the art

Replace a file in `source/` (same name and shape), or point the script at a new one, then run `python3 tools/dev-assets.py`. Square art that fills a wide or tall slot is placed whole, and the rest of the slot is filled with a blurred extension of it, so titles baked into the art stay readable. Nothing is ever upscaled.

To change a video, put its Cloudinary URL in `hosted-media.json`; the cropped and re-encoded versions are built from it in `portfolio.config.js`.

Some artwork shows real company logos (the clients key rack, "certified" badges, product names). The site labels that content as sample; keep the labels, or replace the art, before publishing a real portfolio.
